## The rule

**Contributions must be strictly unconditional, with ZERO benefits flowing back
to the contributor.** No perks, no tiers, no early access, no merch, no
named-sponsor advertising, no bargained-for recognition.

## Why it is load-bearing

This one rule protects **two** tax positions **at once**:

1. **Income characterisation.** IRAS's published position: payments that are *not
   voluntary gifts and are paid in return for benefits* are **business receipts →
   taxable** (s.10(1) Income Tax Act 1947). Keeping contributions benefit-free
   preserves the (already weak, but worth protecting) argument that a receipt is a
   non-taxable gift rather than trade income.
2. **GST scope.** Genuine **unconditional** contributions are **outside the scope
   of GST** and are **excluded** from the **SGD 1,000,000** registration-threshold
   count. A *mere acknowledgement or small token of appreciation is not a direct
   benefit* — so a name on the wall is fine. **Naming/logo/advertising for sponsors
   IS a direct benefit → a taxable supply** → it both becomes trade income **and**
   counts toward the SGD 1M GST threshold.

**The moment any benefit flows back, both positions collapse together.** That is
why this is a product constraint, not a footnote.

> GST rate is **9%**; corporate tax is a flat **17%** (less SUTE/PTE exemptions).
> Reserve against undeployed funds and keep inflow/spend reasonably matched.

## What this means for what we've already built

- **Supporter wall (name-only, equal weight, no amount):** ✅ compliant. Counsel
  confirmed a name on the wall is *recognition, not a bargained-for advertising
  service*. The #36 "named ≠ priced" design (no amounts, no ranking, equal weight)
  keeps it firmly on the safe side. Keep it that way.
- **Org / partner strip (`OrgPartnerStrip`):** ⚠️ **watch zone.** Equal-weight,
  relationship/opt-in, never amount-gated — that design is deliberately defensive.
  But displaying an **organisation's name/logo** edges toward "named-sponsor
  recognition," which counsel flags as the danger zone for *advertising benefit*.
  **Guardrail:** the strip must read as *acknowledgement* ("organisations standing
  with us"), never as *advertising sold in exchange for a contribution*. No logo
  placement promised as consideration, no tiered/sized logos, no "sponsored by."
  **[CONFIRM-CA]** that pure equal-weight acknowledgement of in-kind partners is
  not a taxable supply before any org strip goes live with real partners.
- **No contribution tiers / suggested-amount perks.** Suggested fiat amounts are
  fine (friendly, no benefit attached); they must never unlock anything.

## Enforcement

Any future feature that gives a contributor *anything* in return for contributing
(badges that confer access, merch, sponsor advertising, perks) must be treated as
**re-opening the tax characterisation** and routed through Legal + CA **before**
build. Default answer is **no**.
